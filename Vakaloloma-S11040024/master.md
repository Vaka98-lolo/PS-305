# PS 305 Health Psychology - "Understanding Health and Illness through Stress and Coping Theories"
Wong, T., & Shek, D. T. L. (2025). Stress and coping strategies of Hong Kong university students during the COVID-19 pandemic: A qualitative study. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health.
This study examined the stress and coping strategies used by Hong Kong university students during the COVID-19 pandemic. The researchers conducted focus group discussions with 56 university students and used thematic analysis based on the Transactional Theory of Stress and Coping. The findings showed that students experienced several challenges, including negative emotions, health-related anxiety, family conflict, and difficulties with online learning and career development. Students coped with these challenges through strategies such as seeking social support, problem-focused coping, emphasizing positive thinking, and tension reduction. The study concludes that understanding students’ coping strategies can help educators and policymakers support university students’ mental health during crises.
